## External knowledge, not refreshed in this pass

In the United States sports wagering became a matter for the states after the Supreme Court struck down the federal ban in *Murphy v. NCAA* (2018). Operators are licensed state by state and sportsbooks limit accounts they judge sharp. Event-contract exchanges regulated federally have listed sports contracts, and their status against state law has been contested in court. A bettor's edge is measured against the closing line. These facts bear on the speedrun and must be refreshed by a research pass before real stakes are placed; this pass did not search the web.
