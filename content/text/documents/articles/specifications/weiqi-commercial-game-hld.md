# Weiqi — one genuine game, one authoritative record, shared suppliers

**Decision status:** candidate high-level architecture (Stage 3), NOT independent SRS admission, accepted HLD, low-level design, native fabrication, a successful test, deployed online play or a customer sale. This HLD consumes the original Game Systems Work requirement, the [Weiqi Commercial Game SRS](weiqi-commercial-game-srs.md), Causeway Public Games' customer and profit contract and Lenticule's current Weiqi market study. The full five-stage engineering sequence remains requirements → independently admitted specification → architecture → detailed design → native Data/Code fabrication. The current SRS is itself still a candidate; the competent Red Cup and Causeway receivers must review both it and this HLD before detailed design is admitted.

## 1. System and receiver decision

The **product** is an entertaining competitive game with a recoverable real Go match, not principally a reference work or a collection of UI buttons. A representative complete encounter is: enter an authentic 9×9 fight within two actions; feel convincing placement, defense, capture and resolution; meet a recognizable opponent; replay, answer an actual sect invitation, challenge again and select optional high-value entertainment or teaching experiences. A legitimate buyer must be able to receive an authorized paid benefit from whichever commercial product Causeway selects—courses, campaigns, creator content, institution licenses, clubs, events or analysis. The $2.99 Deep Review is **not** the designated primary business or mandatory first sale. Cross-device agents/character identity and fulfilled merchant/institution transactions require independently received providers, not source assertions.

**Selection B — retained Weiqi rules authority plus shared game/Web/commerce:**
- Keep Red Cup weiqi-engine as the sole Go rules, game history, score, settlement, semantic-event and SGF authority. Retain the single existing weiqi-web app as the browser consumer and preserve its honest offline/local mode.
- Evolve the existing unpublished Weiqi durable-match source into an admitted single-writer match service, backed by actual authenticated human/agent principals and durable replay. Do not activate its old capability-link-only Worker merely because the file exists.
- Consume Qwzspace presence/reconnection, scene projection, tween, celebration, render loop and accessible text/2D view; Cast's generic agent-runner; Resonator's genuine WebAudio cue mixer; Go GTP and KataGo/GNU Go specialist engine; Footing's replay-verified competition; and the *actual* native Red Cup Stripe, Digital Goods and group financial settlement providers. Each is optional until selected, installed, measured and received; do not hand-copy its code into the Weiqi app.
- Backend capacity, rights, paid-result work, regulatory permission, eligible merchant and source-selected hosting are distinct supplier qualifications. No imaginary configured:false facade, second ledger, shadow wallet, duplicated checkout, local-only reward, new standalone engine, spare deployment manifest or compatibility shim is a viable substitute.

**Alternatives compared:** (A) just polish the static local client costs least and keeps offline access but cannot meet cross-device rivalry, independent rank, strong agents or legitimate paid content; retain only as a supported degraded/free mode. (C) let Qwzspace separately adjudicate and own another complete Go intent log creates two contradictory game histories and fails idempotency and maintainability; use Qwzspace for presentation, social scene and transport, not a second Go rules store. (D) make ordinary public Go use the testnet FOCG arena imports wallet, latency, gas and potentially regulated betting into the first match; preserve the testnet agent/FOCG business as a separate qualified product.

## 3. Transaction and user-experience architecture

**One move:** An actor authenticated to the match submits the match reference, expected version, distinct command id and legal candidate command. The existing match engine verifies principal, version, turn, superko and scoring state; a repeated exact idempotent request returns the existing accepted result; a reused id with different principal/command refuses. The single-writer host durably appends the accepted move *before* acknowledging and publishing the derived state version, semantic events and hash. Qwzspace/scene and audio subscribers render from this acknowledged source, never from an optimistic local “success” or a duplicated independent room log. Resume after a dropped connection queries the latest verified checkpoint and ordered deltas; replay computes the same end result with the engine. LLD must prove atomic sequencing and no two concurrently accepted contradictory moves.

**Settlement concurrency counterexample:** The actual weiqi-engine match source currently checks expectedVersion against the derived session.version, but a first dead-stone proposal updates pendingSettlement and the ordered Cartridge log without committing an ordinary rule move or advancing session.version. Thus two different proposals with the same expectedVersion may be sequentially accepted even though the match's accepted log and stateHash changed between them. The current match-engine test/match.test.mjs exercises stale ordinary plays, not that two-party settlement sequence. **The HLD cannot assume session.version is a monotone revision of every match admission.** LLD must separate the match-log acceptance sequence/revision from the engine rules-state version and carry the former as the concurrency precondition for *all* commands, including dead-stone proposals. Preserve a legitimate fresh counterproposal and identical-mask confirmation under the actual Go settlement rules; refuse a stale, different proposal until it observes the new accepted envelope state. Verify exact-idempotent duplicate of the first proposal, changed-body duplicate, simultaneous conflicting proposals, a fresh counterproposal, subsequent identical confirmation, replay after host restart and versioned browser observer recovery. Only the competent rules/match source and native fabrication may implement this migration; do not insert a page-side version shim or duplicate log.

**Modern game feel:** An accepted placement has audible and visual weight, an atari signals a genuine threatened group, a capture changes stones and creates a distinct impact, rival reactions correspond to the real accepted move, and final settlement has a coherent result/rematch progression. Use existing Qwzspace tween/celebration and Resonator cues rather than HTML effect snippets. A text/2D accessible view and input remains fully legible without sound, under reduced motion and on lower-end mobile devices. UI animations never adjudicate or delay a legal move. Live and replay both project the exact same engine events. Measure click-to-first-intersection, p95 input acknowledgment, worst cases with captures, frame times, battery/CPU and task completion against a named contemporary competitor, not against an imagined UI aesthetic.

**A genuine rival:** Cast supplies real persona, identity, think/act/reaction stream and a generic transport; the transport calls the same Weiqi match port humans use. A policy uses source-qualified game state and legal moves; a strong one may use Go GTP/KataGo only when the actual host has installed that process, licensed weights, bounded compute and independent strength-calibration matches. On timeout, the game offers an honest recoverable state or an explicitly weaker disclosed offline bot, never a fake stronger agent or a pre-scripted winning match. Match state owns identity and move permissions; Cast owns no second score.

**Real social competition:** Footing receives only an accepted game/attempt replay with host-trusted date, contest seed, principal and unique scored-attempt identity. It enforces a consumed scored attempt and canonical server recomputation. A local Puzzle Rush timer or localStorage best is personal practice and cannot be promoted into a globally ranked score or money liability. Reuse in a second real game chosen by its actual consumer and test both; do not create a test-only mimic.

## 4. Profit and monetary architecture

**Optional analytical utility, not a ratified flagship or first sale:** Causeway retained an exploratory one-time $2.99 “Deep Review” of one 9×9/13×13 real game or admissible SGF. That price and SKU remain conditional; two units cost $5.98 and already exceed a competitor's advertised $5.95 monthly Basic tier. The player must still complete and enjoy the full free battle, receive an honest useful preview and immediately rematch regardless of purchase. Customer value is a concept-explained report about *their actual accepted fight*, not a fixed PDF with an arbitrary download token.

**Work-to-customer lifecycle:** A verified accepted match (or replay-validated SGF), legitimate buyer, merchant, price revision, ruleset, model/version/rights and bounded analysis budget name one unique commissioned-work obligation. Native Web Commerce must extend its *shared*, existing authenticated Stripe event/DO entitlement/delivery capability to cover buyer-specific asynchronously generated work: capacity reserved before enabling checkout, admitted and idempotent payment, exact bound analysis job, pending/failure/cancellation/refund, content-identified report, buyer entitlement and authorized second-device retrieval. Generated analysis runs on a qualified process-capable host; the browser Worker is not that host. One repeated payment webhook/work request must never produce a second charge or paid report. Wrong game, owner, model/license, exhausted capacity, forged callback, revocation and failed provider must refuse with a recoverable or compensable state. One real customer receipt, delivered report and independently reconciled merchant ledger settle **this analysis offer's receiving**, not a source-only “armed” button. A school contract, sect campaign, creator teaching course, premium season or licensed club must be evaluated against its own paying customer's legitimate completed benefit and costs, independently of analysis fulfillment.

**Net contribution:** Per eligible cohort the sole true numerator is collectible and settled purchase/sponsor/ads revenue; subtract actually owed rewards, provider and processor/network fees, refundable liabilities, rights/license, model compute/hosting, customer acquisition and support. Preserve denominators and 7/28-day return/repeat purchase measurement, dated prior forecast and chronological held-out observations. A hypothetical $2.99 price, false traffic, passing purchase mock or sponsored ZBD example cannot count as profit.

**Real Lightning/ZBD:** A separately verified eligible accomplishment or sponsored participation can create one exact-value funded financial obligation, not automatically a victory, client point or ENERGY redemption. The enterprise financial owner reserves exact commodity and amount under beneficiary and source evidence; authorized server-side ZBD operation uses the endpoint's documented units and secrets; provider acceptance, settlement, treasury discharge and beneficiary receipt remain separate. Never silently select an available mock in place of explicitly requested ZBD; never return settled based only on an existing ledger id without matching actor, amount, beneficiary, direction and provider. Timeout preserves the uncertain original transfer id for readback, not a second send. Current energy-bank zbdBackend is explicitly non-networked and must not be called a payout provider. Sandbox results cannot be labeled player earnings. Legal/market approval and funded receipt are prerequisites, not reasons to reduce the ambition of the actual playable game.

**Separate IdleGames:** Do not attach a fake idle counter to the Go page. The proper first idle consumer is an existing independently owned idle game with real deterministic resource/work evolution, bounded trusted-clock catch-up, a meaningful player strategic choice and honest persistent progress; select it by actual source/consumer evidence. It may reuse shared presentation, Footing and legitimately qualified payouts, while keeping its game-law events distinct from Go. Measure a genuinely paid/co-funded value loop and attributable margin before claiming an earning product.
