# Weiqi Commercial Game — SRS/SAS

**Product receiver:** Causeway Public Games, operating for the 561 Group's commercial interest; the external customers are players, competitive participants, paying buyers, licensed creators and, only for admitted payout products, eligible reward beneficiaries. **Engineering supplier:** Red Cup Game Systems and its actual upstream Web, Game, Data, Code, Networking and Commerce owners. **Demand authority:** the current [Game Systems original Work requirement](../../../../requirements/games-pressure-upstream-and-the-sports-weiqi-lines.json), particularly R1, R2 and R10–R20. Existing sports-betting and FOCG duties R3–R9 remain separate, not quietly absorbed into this product.

**Admission status:** a source-bound **candidate SRS**, not ratified HLD/LLD, fabricated code, qualified native CI, deployed product, collected income, or customer acceptance. No game package, Stripe/ZBD product, or source pin is automatically enrolled by publication of this specification. This specification defines observable behavior so a qualified architect may assess real packages without inventing the product from their README examples.

## 1. Economic and experiential product boundaries

Weiqi shall be a playable modern game and potentially profitable **game business**, not primarily an education site, a compliance showcase or another local-only score demo. The first coherent receivable product combines: an exciting complete opponent match; event-driven tactile/visual/audio feedback; persistent legitimately received player/game identity and record; compelling next challenge or rematch; and one actual valuable customer-paid benefit. A modern first-session and a paid product must be independently delivered before claims of market parity or profitable operation.

**External competitive evidence at 2026-10-14:** [BadukPop](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.coreplane.badukpop.prod) advertises more than 5,000 curated problems, differentiated AI strengths, ranked practice and worldwide play. [GoMagic's 2026 changelog](https://gomagic.org/changelog/) adds bots, mini-games and live events. These are provider statements and scope benchmarks, **not** observations of Weiqi demand, purchase conversion or relative quality. A competitor's feature list is never a substitute for paired sessions against actual versions.

**System boundary:** received client(s), admitted game/rules record, authenticated actor/character, competition/event authority, game presentation/audio, opponent/analysis provider, commercial offer, purchase and entitlement provider, service installation and independent customer receiver. The original Science glossary remains science-owned content; technical game source remains Red Cup-owned; merchant/customer rights remain Causeway and the actual paying principal's rights; provider credentials and money custody remain with their true operators. Upstream technologies are selected only during HLD admission.

### Deep Review: one optional analysis offer inside Causeway's broader product economy (not SRS admission)

The [source-owned Public Games commercial decision](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-media-services-section/blob/main/services/public-games-services-section/README.md) also retains an **optional proposed paid analysis**: a **single, persistently recoverable Deep Review of one accepted 9×9 or 13×13 game or admissible SGF**, with a **US$2.99 per-review test price, before taxes**. The buying actor must have a legitimate completed match or admitted SGF, receive a prior intelligible free preview, buy **exactly one** independently bounded and charged review through the *existing* Red Cup Stripe/digital-goods contract, and receive one persistent report with rule-valid variation, consequential move explanations linked to the source-owned science glossary, analysis budget/model provenance, replay and export. The game front-end must provide compelling actual battle, feedback, progress and replay around the report; a stand-alone study widget or payment URL would not deliver this particular analysis offer.

The source-owned [Lenticule Weiqi market teardown](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-market-teardown.md) supplies the October 14 primary-source price comparison and counterexample: two purchases at US$2.99 cost US$5.98, exceeding a competitor's advertised US$5.95 monthly Basic tier. The design and receiving must therefore test **occasional-buyer value and repeat-buyer contribution separately**, rather than claiming general price superiority or assuming the subscription alternative can be beaten by aesthetic polish alone.

This optional analysis product narrows **WG-SRS-003/004/005/007/008/009/010** to one coherent *analysis transaction*, but does not constrain entertainment, sect campaigns, classes, learning packs, institutional licensing, creator shops, sponsorships or paid club participation to that SKU. It makes source composition, account entitlement, work identity and completed delivery indispensable *when the analysis offer is sold*, not prerequisites for the other independent commercial products. Design must decide how authorized player identity, accepted game sessions, native checkout/fulfillment and GNU Go/KataGo job outputs reference the *same* durable work and how charges, compute budgets, refunds and provider timeouts preserve that relationship. The analysis/fulfillment provider must refuse an unlicensed model, unprofitable bounded offer, forged game principal or undeliverable review. No interface or record should label an unpaid or failed report delivered.

**Authority boundary:** the price is a buyer-value experiment, not evidence of demand, a confirmed merchant, approved processor terms, rights clearance, selected runner or installed entitlement provider. Independent qualified Red Cup specification authority and Causeway seller/customer receiving remain required. A legitimate paid order, usable product and positive incremental contribution are independent *future acceptance* for each chosen commercial line, not inferred from an analysis hypothesis or source edit. Keep separate on-chain arena, idle game and optional funded ZBD rewards outside the first purchase's state/ledger semantics.

## 2. Source-bound functional specification

Each clause is one observable supplier/receiver behavior and traces to the original requirement; valid variations and refusal cases are not optional documentation.

**WG-SRS-001 — Start a genuinely playable game (R2, R10).** When a new or returning eligible visitor chooses a game from the public entry point, the system shall begin a legal, meaningful 9×9 match or competitive problem challenge in at most **two deliberate actions** without requiring a tutorial or reading a glossary. Valid: a returning player directly rematches on a supported larger board. Refusal: an unavailable or incompatible game mode gives an explicit actionable response, never an inert button or fabricated game. *System receiving:* start-to-first-control observation and one completed result on representative mobile and desktop.

**WG-SRS-002 — Complete the actual rules-governed match (R1, R2, R19).** For every admitted move/pass/resignation/settlement, the system shall use one authoritative Go rules and match-record implementation to determine legality, next state, outcome and independently replayable command history. Valid: Chinese and Japanese 9×9 games with their distinct counting regimes and a rematch against either color. Refusal: bad turn, stale version, ko/superko, unauthenticated principal, conflicting dead-stone proposal and a command after final settlement cannot alter the accepted result. *Receiving:* independent full-game replay, including one captured group, two-pass counting and different-rule counterexample.

**WG-SRS-003 — Give events compelling physical and audible impact (R2, R11).** When a game command is accepted, the delivered client shall render recognizably different anticipation, placement, capture, threat, successful defense, victory and failure responses, with coherent motion, layered audio cues, display priority, mixing and retained player volume controls. These responses shall derive from the actual accepted game state and remain correct under replay; they shall not counterfeit a capture or award. Valid: normal-speed desktop and touch-controlled mobile with sound on/off. Refusal: sound unavailable or reduced-motion preference shall leave the move fully usable and legible without inventing game state. *Receiving:* the same accepted event driving live/replay presentation, actual device listening and rendering tests, and a named current competitor side-by-side.

**WG-SRS-004 — Opponents must be meaningful rivals (R12, R19).** When a player selects an opponent, the system shall present an identifiable opponent and a play policy with measurable legal game performance, differentiated by actual strategy/strength rather than cosmetic tier labels. Valid: a novice-appropriate teaching opponent and a genuinely stronger opponent with bounded analysis compute and visible response. Refusal: uninstalled engine, over-budget inference, stale opponent record or connection failure shall result in a recoverable/retryable state; neither a nonexistent player nor a scripted strength ranking shall be advertised as real. *Receiving:* reproducible full match series and independent expert or external engine calibration for each marketed ability.

**WG-SRS-005 — Persistent rivalry and social identity (R12, R13).** When an authorized player returns, the service shall recover permitted account/character, rivalry and accepted game history across legitimate sessions, allowing human and agent players through the same game admission contract while retaining their distinguishable participant types. Valid: two independent sessions of one actor, and a named agent legitimately returning from another group game. Refusal: another principal must not resume, spend or rewrite the first player's match or account. *Receiving:* independent login/recovery, provenance of moves and meaningful returning-opponent continuity; mere `localStorage` or a URL fragment is not cross-session authenticated history.

**WG-SRS-006 — Meaningful competition and tournament records (R13, R17).** Whenever the product advertises a ranked or scored challenge, the service shall admit a server-verifiable challenge definition, user/agent attempt, rule-valid result, outcome timestamp authority, uniquely claimable attempt identity and reproducible rank. Valid: one scored daily challenge, a separate practice run and an authorized leaderboard retrieval from another device. Refusal: duplicate score, client-altered timer, forged seed, replayed achievement, unauthorized actor, and conflicting simultaneous submissions cannot create extra ranked or paid claims. *Receiving:* independent game replay and score authority plus concurrency/failure injection. The existing browser Puzzle Rush timer is *not* this authority.

**WG-SRS-007 — Playable progression and next challenge (R10, R13, R20).** After a resolved game or challenge, the system shall reveal the actual result, record legitimate progress, offer a meaningfully different replay/rematch/next encounter and make optional learning/review available without forcing it as the primary product interaction. Valid: a player loses and tries a revised opponent or next tactical challenge. Refusal: a not-yet-received event shall not unlock a priced entitlement, global rank or cash claim. *Receiving:* observed repeat-session task and valid progress recovered from the accepted account.

**WG-SRS-008 — Truthful game analysis as utility (R19, R14).** For a game genuinely admitted to analysis, the service shall offer bounded analysis of legal alternate continuations and, if sold, deliver a persistent independently identifiable requested review with disclosed model/ruleset/resource budget and meaningful accuracy limits. Valid: an SGF/game-record analysis with candidate move/ownership assessment and an export a customer can reopen. Refusal: malformed SGF, inconsistent game state, unlicensed model, exhausted compute budget, uncertain provider or unpaid premium entitlement does not yield a mislabeled premium review. *Receiving:* cross-check against actual Go analysis engine and complete buyer delivery, including error and retriable outcomes.

**WG-SRS-009 — Native, fully delivered paid game benefit (R14, R15, R20).** When a qualified merchant publishes a priced game offer, an eligible buyer shall be able to examine rights and price, pay through the established house commerce provider and consume the purchased benefit from the same legitimate game account or recipient entitlement. Valid: paid game-review or creator content fulfillment, with provider-confirmed payment, unique entitlement and working delivery. Refusal: duplicate callback, unpaid/failed charge, mismatched currency/mode, incorrect principal, unavailable fulfillment, refund or revoked grant must not award unauthorized permanent access. *Receiving:* real source-owned Stripe payment/provider event, durable fulfilled entitlement and customer playback/readback—not a connector screenshot, hand-entered SKU or a local button.

**WG-SRS-010 — Commercial measurement and forecasting (R15).** After a real public session or paid order, the business shall account for attributable completed visits, initiated/completed games, repeat play, offered prices, consented customer purchases, recognized revenue, refunds, platform/processor expense, licensed content/creator obligations, compute/network cost, support, and net contribution for that cohort. Valid: segment-specific dated forecast and later out-of-sample observation of purchases and realized cost. Refusal: an anonymous visit, imagined conversion, staged fake checkout or simulated reward is not counted as revenue/profit. *Receiving:* independently reconciled provider books and forecasts against a baseline and chronological holdout.

**WG-SRS-011 — Conditional real-money participation (R16, R20).** Only where a specifically contracted product, verified participant, permitted jurisdiction, lawful game mechanic and reserved funding are jointly admitted, the service shall support exact-valued BTC Lightning/ZBD earning obligations and independently settled beneficiary payouts from accepted, unique events. Valid: one capped confirmed award, one provider-finalized payment and owner/customer reconciliation. Refusal: duplicate achievement, missing reserve, copied score, ineligible participant, provider timeout, wrong units or unresolved transfer cannot discharge a liability or appear as received player earnings. *Receiving:* actual provider and beneficiary evidence in addition to the authoritative enterprise ledger. This is **not** a prerequisite for an honest nonmonetary playable first rung.

**WG-SRS-012 — Independent idle-game economic line (R20).** When an idle/resource game is selected as a commercial product, the system shall compute genuine bounded autonomous resource transitions and player actions against a repeatable game simulation with explicit time, cost and resource limits. Valid: a paused/resumed session with identical lawfully replayed result and credible next decision. Refusal: wall-clock spoofing, repeated old tick, browser-tab replay and unearned monetary claims cannot advance competitive or real-value obligations. *Receiving:* one legitimate idle-game session and separately measured willing-payer contribution. Do not conflate this product with Go teaching, or fabricate passive Go victories.

**WG-SRS-013 — Commissioned analysis is a paid, independently fulfillable work obligation (R14, R15, R17, R19).** For each offered Deep Review, the system shall establish one identified, cost- and resource-bounded analysis obligation belonging to an eligible purchasing actor and an accepted immutable game/SGF identity. The existing commerce authority shall permit a charge only when licensed analysis capacity, funds/processor authorization, bounded latency and an independently retrievable result-delivery path are available; a successful charge shall bind an entitlement to that *specific* obligation, not just a generic downloadable SKU. Execution shall use the true source-derived game record, preserve model/ruleset/visit-budget provenance, publish one content-identified result and let the same authorized buyer reopen it without a second payment. A retry, duplicate Stripe event, failed worker, expired session, interrupted analysis, refund or revocation shall converge on one original financial/work identity with a verifiable pending, fulfilled, compensated or refused state. *Valid:* one accepted 9×9 match is charged once and its report is retrievable from a second authorized device. *Distinct valid:* one eligible 13×13 imported SGF is replay-verified and analyzed under a separately costed quota. *Refusals:* mismatched match owner, absent capacity/license, a release pointer for an unrelated fixed good, repeat charge for the same work, or paid-but-undeliverable output. *Independent receiving:* native payment and durable entitlement, admitted analysis result bytes, customer reopen/export and financial reconciliation agree on one source identity. **A static digital-goods download or "checkout succeeded" cannot satisfy this clause without the reusable upstream per-work fulfillment capability.**

**WG-SRS-014 — Real-money provider choice and settlement must be fail-closed (R15, R16, R17).** A game or idle-game award that names BTC Lightning/ZBD shall use the competent enterprise payment/treasury provider with authenticated actual merchant permissions, settled reserves, exact integer units and an accepted distinct beneficiary; neither game ENERGY nor a backend's configured flag is money. An explicitly selected monetary provider must be honored or refused: the system shall never silently substitute an in-memory mock, a testnet token, a stub invoice, a different account or another payment instrument. A previously seen settlement identifier shall return a completed outcome *only if* the original principal, award/source, direction, commodity, exact amount, provider, beneficiary and finality evidence match; a conflicting repeated identifier shall refuse, without discharging a new debt. Accepted provider requests, network-finalized settlements, discharged ledger liabilities and actual player receipts are separate states. *Valid:* funded unique earned result and verified beneficiary are settled once with provider-finalized reference and balanced enterprise books. *Distinct valid:* a lawful sponsor-funded idle-game prize follows the same payment authority without masquerading as a Go result. *Refusals:* selected ZBD unavailable, API key merely present, unsupported geography, wrong msat/sat unit, provider timeout, same award paid to a different recipient, distinct payout sharing one ledger ID, or balance existing only in client storage. *Independent receiving:* negative/no-reserve and duplicate/cross-principal cases plus one legitimately settled merchant-and-player payment. **Source-shipped mock and ZBD-shaped backends are never evidence of this capability.**

**WG-SRS-015 — WXWQ / WeiqIdol makes practiced tactics into actual encounters (R1, R2, R10, R12, R17, R19).** When an engine-accepted game has a real capture, escape, ko or connection attributable to a known participant, a named fictional visiting character may offer an optional sect tryout, mission, next opponent or teaching encounter *sourced to that move and position*. Taking the offer must lead to executable legal Go under the same engine, with an objective adjudicated by actual accepted game events, retained opponent/character identity where such an identity service exists, and a source-linked learning route; decline must leave Play usable. The original [WeiqIdol vision](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-weiqidol-product-vision.md), the eight already-authored [Opponent Cast](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-game-system-services-section/services/go-engine-services-section/apps/weiqi-platform/content/text/documents/articles/design/opponent-cast.md) and the house Cast/Runner are incumbent input—not permission to create an unrelated NPC rules engine. *Valid:* observed Black or White move yields a reproducible real challenge; the same original move succeeds and a non-target legal alternative does not automatically pass; accepted result leaves the source match unchanged. *Refusal:* an imported SGF cannot establish human ownership, a cosmetic tier cannot certify national Go tradition, no sect membership arises from a counter, a static fictional line is not a genuinely online AI actor, and an unrelated/malformed source event grants no reward. *Receiver:* one playable signed/rules-admitted challenge and independent player feedback; genuine persisted agents and cross-device recruitment require their separate original provider.

**WG-SRS-016 — Education is an independent commercial product, not a pretext or afterthought (R14, R15, R17, R20).** When public games offer courses, culturally specific Go/style education, coach/creator experiences or institutional instruction, the same accepted Go game and source-owned teaching graph shall furnish complete playable lessons, contextual concepts, student progress and real authorized pedagogy. Real teaching lineage arises from specifically completed consented instruction, distinct from fictional sect membership and documented historical Japanese/Korean/Chinese named schools or practitioner styles. A learner may deliberately enter sourced cultural/language/history content rather than being assigned a nationality or tradition by an insufficient sample of moves. *Valid:* individually paid course and its actual completed exercises; independently paid school/club license and functioning class; fulfilled teacher/creator commission through shared commerce and agreed rights. *Refusal:* static content tile without purchasable utility, fabricated institution seat, unsourced historical attribution, accidental sale of a future course or an unverified badge. *Receiver:* paid entitlement, working learning and merchant/institution/creator reconciliation per offer, separately from optional Deep Review and ZBD.

## 3. Game quality and resource acceptance (R11, R17, R18)

**WG-SRS-Q01 — Cross-device control:** input-to-visible-authoritative-action p95 must be compared against a dated mainstream Go client on at least one contemporary Android touchscreen and desktop browser at comparable board sizes. An initial engineering *candidate* bound is <=120 ms for local move acknowledgment excluding explicitly communicated opponent thinking; the accepted market threshold remains **TBD until measured**. The 9/13/19 boards must all be interactive and readable.

**WG-SRS-Q02 — Performance budgets:** the renderer/audio composition shall keep the interaction available under specified CPU/GPU/network/memory limits, with deliberate degradation to 2D, reduced effects or silence where a device lacks capabilities. A nominal candidate is sustained 60 fps where the device permits, but low-end baseline, measured battery impact and hard release threshold are **TBD**, not silently declared passed.

**WG-SRS-Q03 — Modifiability/reuse:** when a capability is game-generic, one source-owned shared implementation must be consumed by Weiqi and at least one representative existing sibling game, with no maintained local fork, shadow ledger, duplicate Go adjudicator or backcompat-only shim. Game-specific rules remain in the Weiqi engine. *Refusal:* an import listing without runtime receipt is not integration.

**WG-SRS-Q04 — Live availability and recovery:** installation shall compose exact selected source revisions, dependencies, content/provenance, authorization and declared providers. A failed provider, timeout, restart or invalid build cannot silently count as a healthy game. Recover accepted game/paid state through the real persistent provider; compare deployed static assets with source and separately verify the live playable session. Live SLO, target market and operating cost ceiling are **TBD** at the commercial buyer/operations admission.

**WG-SRS-Q05 — Revenue outcome:** the first actual sale must give the buyer a fully working paid benefit and permit reconciliation of gross receipts, fees and marginal cost. Commercial scaling requires demonstrated positive net contribution over a declared time window with a precommitted stop/scale criterion; revenue from another game is not automatically Weiqi profit.

**WG-SRS-Q06 — Comparative product test:** before claiming *meet* or *beat*, observe complete first-use, opponent, puzzle and repeated-use flows in a contemporaneous version of at least one established mobile/browser Go competitor and the installed Weiqi experience, with comparable devices/skill groups and disclosed scenario definitions. Compare functionality *and* delight, latency, reliability, repeat interest and priced willingness-to-pay; design of sample/cohort and acceptable loss remains **TBD** for Science/receiver acceptance.

## 5. Engineering alternatives and current supplier evidence

### Verified incumbent supplier-contract mismatch — 2026-10-14

This is an **engineering feasibility finding from source**, not a shipped change, provider approval or inference that production fails. It qualifies the previously selected commercial slice before HLD admission and records actual disconfirming cases.

1. **Commerce does not yet fulfill an individually generated analysis job.** The [source-owned digital-goods catalog](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-web-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/web-platform-services-section/services/web2-platform-services-section/services/web2-financial-platform-services-section/services/web2-payment-platform-services-section/services/digital-goods-storefront-services-section/src/digital-goods-storefront.mjs) admits fixed goods or a staged release; [paid-release](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-web-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/web-platform-services-section/services/web2-platform-services-section/services/web2-financial-platform-services-section/services/web2-payment-platform-services-section/services/digital-goods-storefront-services-section/src/paid-release.mjs) reads a release pointer under one offering, and [durable-fulfillment](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-web-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/web-platform-services-section/services/web2-platform-services-section/services/web2-financial-platform-services-section/services/web2-payment-platform-services-section/services/digital-goods-storefront-services-section/src/durable-fulfillment.mjs) has checkout- and grant-addressed entitlements. This is real and useful for already-staged digital goods, but does **not** by itself reserve/run/retry a buyer-specific KataGo work order, deliver that actor's result, or recognize failure after collecting payment. The Web Commerce owner must qualify a reusable paid-work service/entitlement/fulfillment operation using those actual checkout/event/DO foundations; Weiqi owns its analysis semantics and report presentation, not a new checkout clone. A preexisting immutable report sold as a fixed file is a *different product* from the selected individual Deep Review.

2. **Lightning is not yet a payout provider.** [Energy Bank's settlement source](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-game-system-services-section/services/energy-bank/src/settlement.mjs) explicitly calls zbdBackend a stub and performs no real send/finality check. Its configured flag can be true when a key string is supplied; resolveBackend may fall back to mock even when the caller named another unavailable backend. The authorizeSettlement existing-ledger-ID early return also does not compare every field of a repeated request with the original payment. Until the competent monetary owner proves equivalent-request idempotency, refuses conflicting requests, uses an actual ZBD contract/unit/readback and enforces exact reserve liability, **no game calls that combination real settled earnings**. Keep mock game-unit economics test-only and cash/Lightning provider custody outside a game's renderer.

3. **Opponents and presentation are real suppliers, not integrated Weiqi effects.** [Weiqi match](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-game-system-services-section/services/weiqi-engine/src/match.mjs) already verifies principal, idempotency, version, accepted action and replay. [Cast runner](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-game-system-services-section/services/cast/src/runner.mjs) already accepts injected persona/policy/transport; [Resonator game](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-game-system-services-section/services/resonator/src/game/index.mjs) already consumes WebAudio scheduled cue events. [Qwzspace](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-web-engineering-services-section/tree/main/apps/qwzspace) has retained room/resume/tween/render/scene infrastructure. The current static Weiqi client still uses local bot/sound/view and a separate, unpublished Durable Object match Worker. The HLD must compare retaining that local-only experience against a *single rules authority with genuinely consumed* shared room, cast, effect, analysis and commerce providers, including explicit state/runtime costs. Import names or copied UI elements alone cannot pass.

4. **Availability and buyer value remain external.** Existing static Worker configuration declares two Cloudflare custom domains, not a funded analysis backend or a live cross-session game. An unexecuted CI job, compiled JavaScript artifact, failed network probe or paid-content hypothesis is not a customer transaction. HLD, LLD and native fabrication remain separately admitted stages; do not handwrite Weiqi-specific implementations while those gates are missing.
