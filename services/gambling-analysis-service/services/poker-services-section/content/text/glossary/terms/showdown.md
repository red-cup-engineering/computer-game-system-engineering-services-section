A showdown in [poker](../../../../../../README.md) is the stage at the end of a [hand](./hand.md) where the remaining players reveal their cards to determine who wins the [pot](./pot.md) [@britannica2026poker]. A hand reaches showdown only if betting does not end it earlier.

Showdown matters because it is only one of two ways a poker hand can end. The other is collective folding by all but one player. This is why poker cannot be reduced to hand-ranking alone: the threat of future showdown influences betting long before cards are actually revealed.

## Related terms

- [hand](./hand.md) - Showdown is the final comparison of the remaining hands.
- [pot](./pot.md) - The showdown determines who takes the pot if betting has not already ended the hand.
- [fold](./fold.md) - Folding can prevent a hand from ever reaching showdown.
