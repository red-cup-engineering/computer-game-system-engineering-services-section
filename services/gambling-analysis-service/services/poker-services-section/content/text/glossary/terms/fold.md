To fold in [poker](../../../../../../README.md) is to abandon the current [hand](./hand.md) and give up any claim to the [pot](./pot.md) [@britannica2026poker]. Folding ends the player's participation in that hand immediately.

Folding is one of poker's core strategic actions because it limits losses. A player does not need to continue just because chips are already committed. Strong poker depends as much on disciplined folding in weak positions as on aggressive betting in strong ones [@sklansky1999].

## Related terms

- [call](./call.md) - Calling continues the hand at the current price instead of abandoning it.
- [raise](./raise.md) - Raising continues the hand while increasing pressure on opponents.
- [showdown](./showdown.md) - Folding prevents the hand from reaching showdown for that player.
