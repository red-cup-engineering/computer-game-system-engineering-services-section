To raise in [poker](../../../../../../README.md) is to increase the current wager [@britannica2026poker]. A raise keeps the player in the [hand](./hand.md) while forcing later players to pay more if they want to continue.

Raising is the action that gives poker much of its strategic depth. It can build the [pot](./pot.md) when the raiser expects to be ahead, pressure weaker ranges into [folding](./fold.md), or represent a stronger hand than the player actually holds. Because a raise changes both information and price, it does more than move chips [@sklansky1999].

## Related terms

- [call](./call.md) - Calling keeps the current price fixed rather than increasing it.
- [fold](./fold.md) - Opponents may fold in response to a raise instead of paying the higher price.
- [pot](./pot.md) - Raises enlarge the pot while also changing its future growth.
