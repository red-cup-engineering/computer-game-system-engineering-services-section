Play, learn, and read — one rules engine

The game explains itself while you play it.

A rules kernel that recognizes weiqi's own vocabulary — atari, capture, ko, connection, eye — as it happens on the board, and an interface that surfaces this section's own glossary the moment each concept becomes relevant. No account required.

⚫⚪

Play

Play normal Chinese-rules weiqi on 9×9, 13×13, or 19×19, as Black or White, against five local AI tiers. Two passes end the game; mark dead stones, then count.

 ◎

Capture Go

A separate first-capture learning game on 7×7. Use it to make liberties, atari, and capture concrete before returning to normal play.

 ▢

Learn

Step through the Weiqi Overview lesson as an interactive board sequence: liberties, atari, and capture, worked out one move at a time.

 ◉

Tsumego

Graded life-and-death problems, every answer checked by the real rules kernel. Reviews are scheduled by spaced repetition, so problems come back right when you're about to forget them.

 📖

Read

The section's own glossary, compiled straight from its markdown source — the same text the hover-cards in Play and Learn draw from.

One graph, three faces

A capture in Play, a step in Learn, and the glossary entry for “capture” in Read are the same concept seen three ways. The rules engine (@red-cup-engineering/weiqi-engine) emits semantic events with a termRef pointing straight at this section's own corpus; this app never hardcodes a definition — every hover-card and glossary entry is compiled from the same markdown at build time.
