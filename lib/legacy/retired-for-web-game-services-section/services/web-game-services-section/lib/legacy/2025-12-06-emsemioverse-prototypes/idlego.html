Design + Plan

Each Go intersection is a trace; stones are recognitions with stabilizer (σ) and drift (Δ). The GFRTU closures govern how resources grow:

  • Sheaf coherence: adjacency shape quality boosts recognition income and helps stones resist drift.
  • Fiber stabilization: σ-level upgrades raise stability flows; Δ introduces drift pressure tied to liberties.
  • Generative closure: sustained stable stones feed a closure gauge that unlocks cells (local toposes) and defect-mode boosts.
  • Weiqi tactics: liberties and local shape drive resource modifiers; infuse stability to prevent captures-by-drift.
  • AOG braid flux: crossing rows/columns of recognitions form spectral squares; weaving that flux injects σ across the whole board.

Rule of Play

Click a trace to select it, then place a stone. Stones earn trace energy and recognition based on liberties. Drift drains stability when a stone is cramped. Keep σ ahead of Δ, reach generative closure to unlock cell bonuses.

Braid lab: aligned stones in both a row and a column spin up braid flux (spectral squares in the AOG). Spend that flux with “weave” to bathe all stones in σ and nudge closure.

Tip: A stone with ≥3 liberties benefits from sheaf coherence; tangled shapes bleed faster. Use the onboarding steps above and run the self-checks if numbers look odd.
