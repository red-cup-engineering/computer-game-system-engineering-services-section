EVE Assist

Decision support · Not gameplay automation

A dashboard and AI research analyst for EVE Online pilots. It reads your character's public and authorized data from CCP's own ESI API, cites exactly where every number came from and when it was retrieved, and stops there. You still fly the ship, place the order, and choose the fight.

Log in with EVE Online

What this is

EVE Assist logs you in through CCP's own SSO — there is no separate password, and logging in is the same act as granting it read access to your character. From there it builds a dashboard of your skill queue, wallet, and standing, and offers an AI analyst you can ask questions of in plain language. The analyst answers by calling the same read-only data tools the dashboard uses, and shows its work: every claim it makes is traceable back to an observation with a timestamp.

What this is not

  • Not an autopilot. It never flies your ship, places an order, or sends a message for you.
  • Not a black box. Every figure in the dashboard and every claim in a chat reply cites the observation it came from and when it was recorded.
  • Not omniscient. When the data is thin or stale, it says so instead of guessing — "I don't know" is a correct answer here.
  • Not required. The dashboard is useful with no AI key at all; the chat analyst is an opt-in layer on top.

How it's built

Provenance, always

Every retrieved fact carries its source, timestamp, and the identifiers used to fetch it — never a bare number.

Authority isn't flattened

An API observation, a wiki page, and a forum post are different kinds of evidence and are never presented as interchangeable.

Bring your own key

AI chat runs on a Gemini API key you supply and control, stored encrypted, never shown back to anyone — including you, after you've saved it.

Your assistant, too

A remote MCP endpoint lets your own AI tooling use EVE Assist's data tools directly, with a revocable token you mint yourself.

Get started

Logging in grants EVE Assist a read-only set of ESI scopes: wallet, skills and skill queue, location, ship type, assets, market orders, and jump clones. Nothing that lets it act on your behalf.
